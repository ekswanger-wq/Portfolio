# Chatbot accessibility: before and after

Trip planning guide

Start by choosing your destination and travel dates. Compare flights across a few sites before booking.

Next, list what you want to see or do each day, and check opening hours in advance.

Pack light, keep copies of key documents, and leave some time unplanned for spontaneity.

Book your first night of lodging before you leave, so you have somewhere to go when you arrive. Save offline maps of the area in case your signal drops.

Set a daily budget for food and activities, and check whether your destination prefers cash or cards. Keep a small amount of local currency on hand.

Finally, share your itinerary with someone you trust, and note the local emergency number and the address of your embassy.
